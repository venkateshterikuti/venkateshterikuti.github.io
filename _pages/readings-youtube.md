# YouTube Channels

Educational YouTube channels that provide excellent content on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, and related topics.

---

<div class="category-container">

<div class="resource-item">
<h3><i class="fab fa-youtube"></i> <a href="https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi&si=-j5TMHzIkQmwfHQC" target="_blank"><strong>3Blue1Brown - Neural Networks</strong></a></h3>
<p>Exceptional visual explanations of neural networks, backpropagation, and deep learning concepts created by Grant Sanderson. His intuitive animations and mathematical visualizations make complex concepts accessible and engaging. The neural networks series is particularly renowned for its clear explanation of how neural networks learn through backpropagation.</p>
</div>

<div class="resource-item">
<h3><i class="fab fa-youtube"></i> <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@AndrejKarpathy" target="_blank"><strong>Andrej Karpathy</strong></a></h3>
<p>Deep learning insights and tutorials from former OpenAI and Tesla AI director Andrej Karpathy. Features detailed tutorials on building neural networks from scratch, GPT implementations, and AI research discussions. Known for his "Let's build" series where he implements models step-by-step, providing invaluable insights into the mechanics of modern AI systems.</p>
</div>

<div class="resource-item">
<h3><i class="fab fa-youtube"></i> <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@WelchLabsVideo" target="_blank"><strong>Welch Labs</strong></a></h3>
<p>High-quality educational videos on machine learning, neural networks, and data science with excellent visualizations. Known for clear explanations of complex mathematical concepts and intuitive visual representations. The channel covers topics ranging from basic statistics to advanced machine learning algorithms with a focus on building intuition.</p>
</div>

<div class="resource-item">
<h3><i class="fab fa-youtube"></i> <a href="https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLujxSBD-JXglGL3ERdDOhthD3jTlfudC2&si=BF3VL8WSurlE8Mc_" target="_blank"><strong>Two Minute Papers - AI & Deep Learning</strong></a></h3>
<p>Quick, engaging summaries of the latest AI research papers presented by Károly Zsolnai-Fehér. Each video distills complex research into accessible 2-3 minute explanations, covering cutting-edge developments in computer graphics, machine learning, and AI. Perfect for staying up-to-date with the rapidly evolving field of AI research.</p>
</div>

<div class="resource-item">
<h3><i class="fab fa-youtube"></i> <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@krishnaik06" target="_blank"><strong>Krish Naik</strong></a></h3>
<p>Comprehensive machine learning and data science tutorials covering everything from basics to advanced topics. Takes a practical approach with real-world projects and industry insights. Content includes end-to-end ML project walkthroughs, deployment strategies, and career guidance for data science professionals.</p>
</div>

</div>
