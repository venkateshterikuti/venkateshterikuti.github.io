<h3><i class="fas fa-database"></i> <a href="https://huggingface.co/datasets/olewave/OleSpeech-IV-2025-EN-AR-100" target="_blank"><strong>OleSpeech-IV-2025-EN-AR-100</strong></a></h3>
<p>Large-scale multilingual conversational speech dataset featuring multispeaker recordings with speaker diarization capabilities. Contains English and Arabic content from diverse sources including podcasts, talk shows, and teleconferences. Includes human-refined transcripts with fine-grained timestamps, speaker labels, and confidence scores generated using the proprietary Olign alignment pipeline.</p>
