A lightweight, evolving list of papers/articles worth revisiting. This collection includes research papers, technical articles, and resources that have influenced my work in data science, machine learning, and LLMs.

- **Attention Is All You Need** — `Transformers` `NLP`  
  The foundational paper that introduced the Transformer architecture, revolutionizing natural language processing. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

- **BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding** — `NLP` `Pre-training`  
  Groundbreaking work on bidirectional encoder representations that set new standards for language understanding tasks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04805)

- **Language Models are Few-Shot Learners** — `LLM` `GPT-3`  
  The GPT-3 paper that demonstrated the power of scale in language models and few-shot learning capabilities. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165)

- **Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition** — `Computer Vision` `ResNet`  
  Introduction of residual connections that enabled training of very deep neural networks. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385)

- **Visualizing Data using t-SNE** — `Visualization` `Dimensionality Reduction`  
  Essential technique for visualizing high-dimensional data in lower dimensions while preserving local structure. [Paper](https://jmlr.org/papers/v9/vandermaaten08a.html)

- **XGBoost: A Scalable Tree Boosting System** — `Machine Learning` `Gradient Boosting`  
  The paper behind one of the most successful machine learning algorithms for structured data. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.02754)

- **Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS) Proceedings** — `Conference` `Research`  
  Annual collection of cutting-edge research in machine learning and computational neuroscience. [Website](https://neurips.cc/)

- **Distill.pub** — `Visualization` `Explainability`  
  Outstanding visual explanations of machine learning concepts and research findings. [Website](https://distill.pub/)

*This list is regularly updated with new discoveries and insights. Each entry includes practical takeaways or techniques that have proven valuable in real-world applications.*
